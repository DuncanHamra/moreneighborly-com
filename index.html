Metta Meditation

Loving Kindness (metta) is the practice of intentionally sending warmth and goodwill — first where it comes naturally, then rippling outward. The more you practice, the wider the circle grows.

You decide where to start based on what feels right for you. There's no wrong order — just honest practice.
